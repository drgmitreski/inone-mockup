Common questions

Do I need a referral?

For a Medicare rebate, yes: you’ll need a request from your doctor. Without one you can still be scanned, but you’ll pay the full fee with no rebate. We’ll tell you which applies when you book.

When do I pay?

On the day, at the front desk. If your scan is Medicare-rebateable, we lodge your claim on the spot and the rebate goes straight back to your bank account.

What if my scan changes on the day?

We’ll never add a fee without telling you first. If a radiologist recommends something extra, we explain the cost and you decide before we proceed.

Why is screening priced differently?

Screening scans aren’t covered by Medicare, so there’s no rebate. We quote the full fee by phone when you enquire, and nothing is booked or charged until you say yes.

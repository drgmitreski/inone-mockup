Common questions

Which scans are bulk billed?

Most CT, ultrasound, X-ray, DEXA and MRI scans, when you have a valid referral and your Medicare card. A gap applies to selected ultrasound and CT studies, procedures and screening. We tell you which applies, and the exact amount, when you book.

Do I need a referral?

For a Medicare rebate or bulk billing, yes: you’ll need a request from your doctor. Without one you can still be scanned, but you’ll pay the full fee with no rebate. We’ll tell you which applies when you book.

When do I pay?

If your scan is bulk billed, there’s nothing to pay. If there’s a gap, you pay on the day at the front desk. We lodge your Medicare claim on the spot and the rebate goes straight back to your bank account.

What if my scan changes on the day?

We’ll never add a fee without telling you first. If a radiologist recommends something extra, we explain the cost and you decide before we proceed.

Why is screening priced differently?

Screening scans aren’t covered by Medicare, so there’s no rebate or bulk billing. We quote the full fee by phone when you enquire, and nothing is booked or charged until you say yes.

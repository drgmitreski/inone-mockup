MEET THE FOUNDER

Dr Mohammed Alebrahimi

Prime Minister’s Medicare Excellence Award finalistRecognised for excellence in healthcare and passion for patients and community.

Dr Mohammed Alebrahimi is the founder and driving force behind inOne Imaging. With a clinical background spanning allied health and diagnostic services, he identified a critical gap in Melbourne’s outer suburbs — communities underserved by quality, affordable imaging — and built inOne Imaging to fill it.

Recognised at the Prime Minister’s Medicare Awards for demonstrating excellence in healthcare and passion for patients and community. Under his leadership, inOne Imaging has established state-of-the-art clinics across Melbourne, delivering bulk-billed CT, ultrasound, X-ray, DEXA and interventional radiology with fast turnaround and compassionate care.
